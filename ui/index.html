How This Works

Click a key or knob control, then type an action or choose a preset. Save writes the mapping file. Upload sends the saved mapping to the connected keypad.

Use Layer 1 for real programming. We can write mappings for layers 2 and 3, but we do not have a working way to switch the keypad into those layers. Until that changes, layers 2 and 3 are not practically accessible from the device.

After uploading, press the keys on the pad to test them. The Last Upload panel confirms whether the programmer reached the device and exited cleanly.
